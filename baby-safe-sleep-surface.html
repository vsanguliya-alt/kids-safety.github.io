Baby Safe Sleep Surface: A Parent&#x27;s Guide

A safe sleep surface is one of the most important things you can provide for your baby. Following safe sleep guidelines is critical to reduce the risk of Sudden Infant Death Syndrome (SIDS) and prevent injury.

Safe Sleep Guidelines

Place your baby on their back for every sleep, including naps. &quot;Tummy time&quot; is for when babies are awake and someone is watching .

Use a firm, flat mattress with a fitted sheet. This is the only thing that should be in the sleep area .

Keep the sleep area clear of soft objects, pillows, blankets, bumper pads, and toys .

Avoid overheating. Dress your child in light sleepwear and keep the room at a comfortable temperature .

Have your baby sleep in your room for at least the first six months. Your baby should sleep close to you, but on a separate surface designed for infants, such as a crib or bassinet (Source: NIH).

What to Avoid

Do not use a regular adult bed for a baby.

Avoid soft surfaces like adult mattresses, sofas, or armchairs.

Do not use sleep positioners or wedges.

Never sleep with your baby in an adult bed, on a couch, or in a chair.

Do not smoke during pregnancy or allow anyone to smoke near your baby .

When to See a Doctor

If you are worried about your baby&#x27;s sleep or development, call your health care provider right away.
