Safe Sleep Surface for Your 3-Year-Old

At 3 years old, your child is likely sleeping in a toddler bed or a standard single bed. A safe sleep surface remains crucial to prevent injury and ensure restful sleep. While the risk of SIDS is no longer a concern, falls from bed are a common injury at this age.

The Importance of a Safe Sleep Surface

A safe sleep surface is a firm, flat, and stable area designed specifically for sleep. For a 3-year-old, this is typically a toddler bed or a standard single bed with safety rails.

Safe Sleep Guidelines for a 3-Year-Old

Use a firm, flat mattress with a fitted sheet. This is the only thing that should be in the sleep area.

Keep the sleep area clear of soft objects, pillows, blankets, and toys to reduce the risk of suffocation.

Avoid overheating. Dress your child in light sleepwear and keep the room at a comfortable temperature.

Use a bed rail to prevent falls from a standard bed.

When to Transition to a Standard Bed

Most children transition from a crib to a toddler bed between 18 months and 3 years of age. A toddler bed is essentially a small bed with a low profile and side rails to prevent falls. The transition should be based on your child&#x27;s readiness, not just age.

Signs your child may be ready:

Climbing out of the crib.

Showing interest in a &quot;big kid&quot; bed.

Reaching the height or weight limit for the crib.

What to Avoid

Do not use a regular adult bed for a 3-year-old without proper safety rails.

Avoid soft surfaces like adult mattresses, sofas, or armchairs.

Do not use sleep positioners or wedges.

When to See a Doctor

If you are worried about your child&#x27;s sleep or development, call your health care provider right away.
