What Parents Should Know

If you suspect your 9-month-old has swallowed a button battery, do not wait for symptoms to appear. This is a medical emergency.

1. Call Emergency Services Immediately

Call emergency services immediately. Do not delay.

2. Do Not Induce Vomiting

Never try to make your baby vomit, and do not give them anything to eat or drink unless specifically instructed by a medical professional.

3. Do Not Perform the Heimlich Maneuver

The Heimlich maneuver is for choking, not for a battery that has been swallowed and passed to the esophagus. Trying it could cause more damage.

4. Go to the Emergency Room

Take your baby to the nearest emergency department.

How to Prevent Battery Ingestion

Prevention is the best strategy. Keep all devices with button batteries out of your baby's reach and sight. Secure battery compartments with tape to make them harder to open, and never leave loose batteries around the house.
