General Principles of Safe Storage

While the sources do not directly address this topic, general safety practices are always recommended. Medications should always be kept out of sight and out of reach of children. They should never be stored in places where a child can easily access them, such as on countertops or in purses.

What to Avoid

Do not store medicines in places that are easily accessible to children.

Avoid keeping medicines in places where they might be mistaken for food or candy.

When to See a Doctor

If you are worried about your baby's health, call your health care provider right away.
