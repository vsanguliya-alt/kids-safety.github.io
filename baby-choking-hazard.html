Baby Choking Hazards: A Guide for Parents

Choking is a serious and common risk for young children. Food or small objects can cause choking if they get caught in your throat and block your airway, and young children are at an especially high risk of choking (Source: NIH). As a parent, understanding the dangers and knowing how to prevent them is essential.

What to Avoid

Babies under 12 months are still learning to chew and swallow, and their airways are very small. Certain foods are known to be high-risk choking hazards. These include hard candy, popcorn, whole nuts, and grapes (unless they are cut into small pieces) (Source: NIH).
