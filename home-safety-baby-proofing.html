Home Safety: A Complete Baby-Proofing Guide

Making your home safe is an ongoing process that changes as your child grows . The goal is to anticipate hazards before your child discovers them, allowing them to explore safely.

🛡️ The Most Critical Safety Steps

Every year, many children are injured by hazards that are easily preventable with a few key measures .

🔒 Secure Furniture and Electronics

Anchor all heavy furniture like dressers, bookcases, and TVs to the wall to prevent tip-overs .

Secure the changing table to the wall and never leave a baby unattended on it . Consider changing diapers on the floor to eliminate fall risk entirely .

🧯 Prevent Falls and Burns

Install safety gates at the top and bottom of stairs .

Install window guards or locks and keep furniture away from windows .

Set your water heater to a maximum of 48.8°C (120°F) to prevent scalding .

Use a non-slip mat in the bathtub .

🔌 Lock Away Hazards

Use safety latches on all cabinets and drawers containing dangerous items (cleaners, medicines, knives) .

Store medications and chemicals out of sight in locked cabinets, not just out of reach .

Cover unused electrical outlets with safety plugs .

Replace corded blinds with cordless ones to eliminate strangulation risk .

🔄 Safety is a Continuous Process

Baby-proofing isn't a one-time task; it evolves with your child . A child who can't crawl is safe from stairs, but a toddler presents new risks. Regularly check your home from your child's point of view and adapt your safety measures to their developmental stage. Keep emergency numbers (poison control, pediatrician) handy .
