Safe Sleep Surface for Your 9-Month-Old

A safe sleep surface is one of the most important things you can provide for your 9-month-old. While the risk of Sudden Infant Death Syndrome (SIDS) decreases after 6 months, following safe sleep guidelines remains critical to prevent injury and ensure restful sleep.

Safe Sleep Guidelines

Place your baby on their back for every sleep, including naps. This is the single most effective way to reduce the risk of SIDS .

Use a firm, flat mattress with a fitted sheet. This is the only thing that should be in the sleep area .

Keep the sleep area clear of soft objects, pillows, blankets, bumper pads, and toys .

Avoid overheating. Dress your child in light sleepwear and keep the room at a comfortable temperature .

Have your baby sleep in your room for at least the first six months (Source: NIH).

What to Avoid

Do not use a regular adult bed for a 9-month-old.

Avoid soft surfaces like adult mattresses, sofas, or armchairs.

Do not use sleep positioners or wedges.

Never sleep with your baby in an adult bed, on a couch, or in a chair.

When to See a Doctor

If you are worried about your baby&#x27;s sleep or development, call your health care provider right away.
