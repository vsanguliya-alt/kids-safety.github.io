Safe Sleep Surface for Your 6-Month-Old

A safe sleep surface is one of the most important things you can provide for your 6-month-old. At this age, the risk of Sudden Infant Death Syndrome (SIDS) is still a concern, making it critical to follow all safe sleep guidelines.

The Importance of a Safe Sleep Surface

A safe sleep surface is a firm, flat, and stable area designed specifically for infant sleep. The American Academy of Pediatrics (AAP) recommends using a firm sleep surface, such as a crib mattress covered with a fitted sheet (Source: NIH).

Safe Sleep Guidelines for a 6-Month-Old

Place your baby on their back for every sleep, including naps (Source: NIH).

Use a firm, flat mattress with a fitted sheet. This is the only thing that should be in the sleep area.

Keep the sleep area clear of soft objects, pillows, blankets, bumper pads, and toys (Source: NIH).

Avoid overheating. Dress your child in light sleepwear and keep the room at a comfortable temperature (Source: NIH).

Have your baby sleep in your room for at least the first six months (Source: NIH).

What to Avoid

Do not use a regular adult bed for a 6-month-old.

Avoid soft surfaces like adult mattresses, sofas, or armchairs.

Do not use sleep positioners or wedges.

Never sleep with your baby in an adult bed, on a couch, or in a chair.

When to See a Doctor

If you are worried about your baby's sleep or development, call your health care provider right away.
