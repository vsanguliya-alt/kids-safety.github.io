Safe Sleep Surface for Your 18-Month-Old

At 18 months old, your child is more mobile and may be transitioning from a crib to a toddler bed. A safe sleep surface remains crucial to prevent injury and reduce the risk of Sudden Unexpected Infant Death (SUID), which includes SIDS and accidental suffocation.

The Importance of a Safe Sleep Surface

A safe sleep surface is a firm, flat, and stable area designed specifically for infant and toddler sleep. The American Academy of Pediatrics (AAP) recommends using a firm sleep surface, such as a crib mattress covered with a fitted sheet (Source: NIH).

Safe Sleep Guidelines for an 18-Month-Old

Place your baby on their back for every sleep, including naps (Source: NIH).

Use a firm, flat mattress with a fitted sheet. This is the only thing that should be in the sleep area.

Keep the sleep area clear of soft objects, pillows, blankets, bumper pads, and toys (Source: NIH).

Avoid overheating. Dress your child in light sleepwear and keep the room at a comfortable temperature (Source: NIH).

Have your child sleep in your room for at least the first six months (Source: NIH).

When to Transition to a Toddler Bed

Most children transition from a crib to a toddler bed between 18 months and 3 years of age. A toddler bed is essentially a small bed with a low profile and side rails to prevent falls. The transition should be based on your child's readiness, not just age.

Signs your child may be ready:

Climbing out of the crib.

Showing interest in a "big kid" bed.

Reaching the height or weight limit for the crib.

What to Avoid

Do not use a regular adult bed for an 18-month-old.

Avoid soft surfaces like adult mattresses, sofas, or armchairs.

Do not use sleep positioners or wedges.

When to See a Doctor

If you are worried about your child's sleep or development, call your health care provider right away.
